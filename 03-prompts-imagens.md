# RPG da Milena: prompts de imagem

Prompts para gerar as ilustrações dos locais e do bestiário em outra IA de imagem (ChatGPT,
Gemini, Midjourney, Leonardo etc.). Cada prompt usa só o que a Milena descreveu no caderno; os
números de página são os da `01-transcricao-integral.md`.

## Como usar

1. **Comece cada pedido colando o bloco de estilo** abaixo e, logo depois, o prompt da imagem.
   Assim todas as imagens ficam com a mesma cara, como num livro só.
2. Os prompts estão em inglês porque a maioria das IAs de imagem responde melhor assim. Se a IA
   aceitar português, também funciona traduzido.
3. **Formato:** locais em paisagem (16:9); criaturas em retrato (3:4), bom para carta ou página
   do bestiário. Se a IA tiver opção de proporção, escolha antes de gerar.
4. **Peça sem texto na imagem.** IA costuma escrever letras erradas. Título e nome entram depois,
   na montagem do livro.
5. Gere de 2 a 4 versões de cada e deixe a Milena escolher. Se uma imagem sair boa, peça as
   próximas "no mesmo estilo da anterior".
6. Salve as escolhidas em `imagens/locais/` e `imagens/bestiario/`, com o nome do item (ex.:
   `05-lago.png`, `mula-sem-cabeca.png`).

## Bloco de estilo (colar antes de todo prompt)

```
Style: fantasy tabletop RPG book illustration, semi-realistic digital painting, rich vibrant
colors with a slightly mystical glow, soft painterly brushwork, dramatic lighting, detailed
environment, mix of Greek mythology and world folklore, suitable for a teen audience (adventurous,
a little spooky, no gore). No text, no letters, no watermark, no frame.
```

Se quiser outro estilo, troque só a primeira linha do bloco. Opções:
- aquarela: `storybook watercolor illustration, soft washes, ink outlines`
- anime: `anime fantasy key art, cel shading, vivid colors`
- livro antigo: `vintage bestiary engraving style, parchment tones, hand-drawn ink`

## Parte 1 · Locais (16:9)

### 01. O despertar (p. 3)

```
A young adult standing in a bedroom at dawn, looking into a mirror while rolling up a shirt
sleeve, revealing a glowing star-constellation mark on the forearm, lines of light connecting
the stars like a zodiac constellation. An open mysterious letter in the other hand, a faint
golden light coming through the window. Mood: quiet, magical, the moment destiny calls.
```

### 02. O Museu de Antiguidades (p. 5)

```
Interior of an old museum of antiquities at night, dusty glass cabinets with Greek vases,
ancient statues and bronze relics, moonlight through tall windows. One ancient artifact engraved
with a crab symbol glows softly blue in the center, as if calling someone. Mood: mysterious,
silent, a secret about to be revealed.
```

### 03. A chegada ao mundo espiritual (p. 5)

```
An ethereal spirit world forest: giant ancient trees with faintly glowing bark, floating
particles of light, mist between the roots, a sky with two pale moons and visible
constellations in daylight. A small group of four travelers seen from behind, arriving
through a shimmering portal of light. Mood: wonder and unease.
```

### 04. A montanha e os portões do santuário do Olimpo (p. 10 e 11)

```
An enormous rocky mountain path climbing toward Mount Olympus, steep cliffs and clouds below,
and at the top colossal golden temple gates with Greek columns, carved with the twelve zodiac
symbols. The gates glow with light as if they could speak. Tiny climbers on the cliff for
scale. Mood: epic, divine, intimidating.
```

### 05. O lago e a "ilha" (p. 13)

```
A huge dark lake, at least a kilometer wide, under stormy clouds, surrounded by forest. A long
giant wooden bridge leads to a small island in the center, and the island is starting to rise
out of the water, with giant spikes emerging from it, because it is actually the back of a
colossal lake monster. A weathered wooden sign at the shore. Mood: tension right before the
battle.
```

### 06. A cabana do barqueiro (p. 15)

```
At the far end of a misty lake, a small crooked wooden cabin with a rowboat tied to a dock. A
hooded figure in a long dark cloak stands at the doorway, face hidden in shadow, holding a
lantern. Mood: ominous, a curse is about to be spoken.
```

### 07. O campo do arco-íris (p. 15 e 16)

```
A wide open meadow with no trees, gentle green hills, small ponds of crystal water on the left
side with a few unicorns grazing peacefully. A bright rainbow arcs across the sky and ends just
behind one of the hills, where an enormous cauldron overflowing with gold coins shines so
intensely that it is almost blinding. Mood: magical, cheerful, suspicious.
```

### 08. O pasto da vaca e o OVNI (p. 17)

```
A huge open pasture at sunset. A cow with small white pigeon wings is being lifted into the
sky by a green tractor beam coming from a classic flying saucer UFO above. Four adventurers
below stare in disbelief. Mood: absurd and funny, a totally random event.
```

### 09. A caverna à noite (p. 18)

```
A rocky cave at night in the spirit world, a small campfire inside, four travelers sleeping
wrapped in cloaks. Outside the cave entrance, a very long dusk sky turning dark purple. Inside,
the shadows on the cave walls are subtly twisting into nightmare shapes. Mood: cozy turning
creepy.
```

### 10. A clareira do grifo (p. 19)

```
A forest clearing surrounded by tall trees, not yet an open field. In the center, a griffin
about 3.5 meters tall resting on the ground, a shiny golden dog tag hanging from a cord near
its talons. Sunbeams cut through the leaves. Mood: majestic, dangerous, could become a friend
or a foe.
```

### 11. A espada na pedra (p. 21)

```
A dense, closed part of an ancient forest, roots and moss everywhere. In the middle, a stone
pedestal with ancient carved inscriptions, and a legendary sword thrust deep into the stone,
glowing with a soft silver-blue light. Light falls on it from a gap in the canopy. Mood:
sacred, only the truly worthy may lift it.
```

### 12. O templo da Hidra (p. 20)

```
Interior of a gigantic ruined Greek temple, broken marble columns, shallow dark water covering
the floor, torches burning green. In the depths, the silhouette of a colossal seven-headed
hydra rising, its eyes glowing. Small heroes at the entrance for scale. Mood: final boss,
overwhelming.
```

### 13. O retorno ao Olimpo (p. 20)

```
The golden gates of the Mount Olympus sanctuary opening wide in a burst of divine light. Four
heroes stand before them: three marked with glowing zodiac constellations on their arms, and
the fourth awakening, surrounded by a swirling aura of water and moonlight with a crab
constellation forming above. Mood: triumphant, the tournament is about to begin.
```

## Parte 2 · Bestiário (3:4)

Em todas as criaturas, se quiser fundo neutro para recortar, acrescente no fim:
`full body, centered, plain dark parchment background`.

### Mula sem cabeça (p. 22)

```
A powerful brown mule with no head: in place of the head, a magical ball of fire that seems
impossible to extinguish, flames roaring upward. It stands guard over a farm field at night,
protecting the animals. Hooves sparking. Mood: fearsome guardian, not evil.
```

### Robin Hood (p. 23)

```
A legendary outlaw archer of the forest, dressed mostly in green with a hooded cloak, leather
quiver, longbow drawn, a dagger at the belt. Sharp eyes, half in shadow under the hood,
standing on a thick tree branch. Mood: skilled, mysterious, morally grey.
```

### Boitatá (p. 24)

```
A giant serpent made of living flames, from Brazilian folklore, coiling through the densest
part of a burned forest. Its body is a charred, glowing ember skin with fire running along it,
eyes like bright coals, venomous fangs. It guards the forest against lumberjacks. Mood:
tragic and furious, a spirit of the forest.
```

### Nessie (p. 25)

```
The colossal lake monster rising from a stormy lake: long neck, huge head with a mouth full of
giant fangs, a back covered in enormous spikes that looked like an island. Water pouring off
its body, waves crashing. Something golden glints on its tongue. Mood: ancient, overwhelming,
but not purely evil.
```

### Ageláda, a vaca (p. 26)

```
A sacred-looking white cow with small white pigeon wings, standing firmly in a pasture and
blocking the path, calm but stubborn expression, a faint holy glow around it, a golden dog tag
on a cord around its neck. Mood: funny but surprisingly powerful boss.
```

### Fobétor, o senhor dos pesadelos (p. 27)

*Esta é uma criatura original no lugar do Freddy Krueger, que é personagem de filme e tem dono.
Mantém o que a Milena escreveu: controla os sonhos, ataca com pesadelo, arranhão, caldeirão e
dardo envenenado. Peça uma criatura original; não peça "Freddy Krueger" nem parecido com ele.*

```
An original nightmare spirit from Greek mythology, son of the god of sleep: a tall, thin
shadowy figure made of dark smoke and fragments of dreams, with long clawed fingers, a pale
porcelain mask with empty eyes, wearing a tattered robe that dissolves into mist. Around it,
a dreamscape bends and twists: floating stairs, a bubbling cauldron, poison darts hovering in
the air. Mood: creepy dream logic, unsettling but not gory.
```

### Grifo (p. 19 e 28)

```
A majestic griffin about 3.5 meters tall: body of a lion, head, wings and front talons of a
golden eagle, feathers in bronze and gold tones, fierce intelligent eyes. Wings half open,
standing in a forest clearing. Mood: noble and dangerous.
```

### Kallikántzaros, o Leprechaun (p. 16 e 29)

```
A small mischievous creature dressed in green clothes with clover-leaf details, wearing a green
top hat with a relatively large golden four-leaf clover pendant. Sitting on top of a huge
cauldron of gold coins, sly grin, holding a coin between its fingers, a small hammer at its
belt. Mood: charming at first glance, clearly not trustworthy.
```

### Excalibur (p. 21)

```
A legendary sword stuck in a stone, seen up close: an ornate silver blade with ancient runes,
a golden hilt with a deep blue gemstone. A ghostly aura around it shows faint images of
souls and visions, as if the sword can look into whoever approaches. Mood: sacred, a
psychological trial.
```

### Hidra (p. 20 e 30)

```
A colossal seven-headed hydra in a flooded Greek temple: scaly dark green and black body,
seven long serpent necks each with a different snarling head, one head breathing fire. Under
its chest, a faint glowing heart visible through the scales. Mood: the final boss, terrifying,
seemingly endless lives.
```
